# Destination planning defaults

Reviewed on **11 October 2026**. `data/destination-planning.json` contains defaults for all 44 bundled destinations, 184 sightseeing entries and 186 popular activities. Its keys match the bundled library exactly. Existing operator-authored content takes precedence when these defaults are merged.

## Meaning of the numbers

- `hub.km`, `hub.hours` and `hub.hours_max` are **one-way estimates from Srinagar city centre to the named road endpoint**. They are not live traffic results, airport distances, guarantees or straight-line distances. The upper allowance accommodates ordinary slower driving and route variation; severe disruption needs an operator change.
- Every record is marked `basis: planning-estimate`. Even where an official distance matches, the reference point and conservative driving allowances are editorial planning inputs. `hub.note` records published numbers where useful and identifies discrepancies instead of presenting them as identical measurements.
- `transfer_minutes` is additional local movement for a stop beyond the hub drive. It is a conservative allowance rather than an exact stop-to-stop navigation result. Nearby sights share a `cluster`; the scheduler can keep those together without assuming distant valley excursions are adjacent.
- `visit_minutes` and `duration_minutes` are planning allowances for the visit or experience, separate from the hub drive. They are not official opening times or product promises. Linked `sight_names` identify an attraction already covered by an activity so the scheduler can avoid charging both full time allowances for the same visit.
- `arrival_suitable` distinguishes short nearby stops from valley excursions, strenuous walks and longer visits. Checked places remain in the programme; timing metadata determines their day assignment and any operator planning estimates.
- For **Lidderwat, Tulian Lake, Kausar Nag, Gangabal Lake, Sheshnag and Amarnath Cave**, the road ends at a trailhead or pilgrimage base. `trail_hours` is a conservative **one-way walking allowance**, additional to the drive. For Kausar Nag it is cumulative walking over several stages. These records must not be used as ordinary motorable day visits. The non-road access type is deliberately retained even when there is road access to the starting village.

## Conflict prevention

The planning data is input to one ordered scheduler. Standard days use 08:00–22:00, adjusted for actual arrival/departure; the selected route determines driving; quoted experiences reserve time; all ticked sightseeing places are then distributed once across the destination's visit days. An overnight base is separate from the sightseeing destination. On an outlying destination's return to Srinagar, remaining sightseeing belongs to that outlying destination rather than automatically adding a Srinagar circuit.

Sight durations and arrival suitability guide assignment, not automatic removal. A long transfer receives nearby stops when a full visit day is available; the latter receives major excursions. An overfull selected sightseeing programme is retained with a nonblocking planning estimate for the operator. Real impossible transfers and priced activities with no usable day remain explicit checks. Paid-activity pricing no longer acts as a hidden second switch for an explicitly ticked sightseeing place; `requires_activity` only identifies shared experience time/copy to avoid counting it twice. The operator controls sightseeing ticks, activity inclusion/pricing and editable day descriptions separately. Narrative and PDF rendering trust that assigned programme without another eligibility pass.

Morning-only market visits and sunrise products use an editorial 06:00–08:00 window. These visits need a dedicated local morning and are never allocated to an afternoon arrival or a road-transfer day. The operator can edit visit windows under advanced timing; these are planning inputs rather than verified opening hours. A general sunrise-or-sunset shikara product does not independently imply a floating market visit.
